About North Star Bakery

Story of the Bakery

North Star Bakery started in 2019 as a weekend table at our neighborhood farmers market. After regular customers kept asking where they could buy our bread during the week, we opened our corner shop on North Star Avenue so we could bake for the community year-round.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We buy our flour, butter, eggs, and seasonal fruit from local family farms and regional mills whenever possible. Working with nearby growers keeps our ingredients fresh and supports our local economy.

Staff Highlights

  • Sarah Jenkins (Head Baker): Runs our early-morning sourdough and hearth bread bakes.
  • David Morales (Pastry Baker): Makes our daily croissants, danishes, and custom cakes.
  • Megan Ross (Front Counter Manager): Helps customers with pre-orders, catering boxes, and allergy questions.
