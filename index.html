Welcome to North Star Bakery

We're a neighborhood bakery making fresh bread, morning pastries, and custom cakes from scratch every day for local families, commuters, and community events.

What We're Known For

Our kitchen bakes small batches early every morning. Neighbors stop by on their way to work for warm sourdough loaves, cinnamon rolls, and butter croissants made with simple local ingredients.

Featured Item of the Week

This week's featured bake is our Honey Cinnamon Brioche Loaf. We bake a limited batch Tuesday through Sunday, or you can reserve one ahead of time on our contact page.
